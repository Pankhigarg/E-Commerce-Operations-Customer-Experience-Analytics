# E-Commerce-Supply-Chain-Performance-Analytics

## 📌 The Business Problem
Olist, a major Brazilian e-commerce platform, was experiencing fluctuating customer satisfaction scores. This project analyzes over 100,000 anonymized orders to identify the root causes of negative reviews, map the geographic distribution of revenue, and expose critical vulnerabilities in the logistics and delivery pipeline.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
* **Data Engineering & EDA:** Python (Pandas, NumPy), SQL Server
* **AI Integration:** Groq API (LLM for automated executive summaries)
* **Business Intelligence:** Power BI, DAX, Power Query

---

## ✨ Features

- Built an end-to-end analytics pipeline using Python, SQL Server, and Power BI.
- Engineered a star-schema data model for enterprise reporting.
- Developed 10 SQL business queries for operational analysis.
- Created interactive Power BI dashboards with DAX measures and synced slicers.
- Automated executive insight generation using Groq LLM integration.
- Identified critical business risks affecting customer satisfaction and retention.

---

## 🤖 AI-Generated Executive Summary

*These insights were automatically generated by passing Python EDA summary statistics to the Groq API, replicating the architecture of enterprise Copilot systems.*

### Revenue Insights
- Olist generated 15.45M BRL across 97K orders with an Average Order Value of 159.80 BRL.
- Revenue is highly concentrated geographically, with São Paulo (SP) driving the vast majority of volume.
- Health & Beauty and Watches & Gifts emerged as the most profitable categories.

### Delivery & Operations Insights
- The on-time delivery rate stands at 93.23%, while 6.77% of orders experienced delays.
- Late deliveries averaged 10.62 additional days before reaching customers.
- Remote northern states (RR, AP, and AM) experienced the highest logistical friction.

### Customer Intelligence
- Customer retention was critically low at just 3.09%.
- Customer satisfaction dropped from 4.3 stars to below 2.0 once delays exceeded four days.
- Delivery performance emerged as a key driver of customer churn.

### Seller & Product Risks
- Quadrant analysis exposed "Dangerous Categories" generating revenue while damaging loyalty.
- Office Furniture and Security & Services recorded the lowest satisfaction scores.
- These categories represent the highest-priority areas for operational intervention.

## 🧠 Key Technical Engineering Feats
* **Resolved DAX Circular Dependencies:** Pushed complex chronological sorting logic upstream to Power Query to ensure stable data modeling for operational delay buckets.
* **Granular Filtering Logic:** Utilized `AVERAGEX` and strict boolean filtering (`is_late = True`) to calculate true operational delay metrics without skewing baseline logistics data.
* **Strategic Quadrant Mapping:** Engineered customized scatter plots with hard-coded operational baselines (Revenue & Satisfaction) to instantly isolate high-risk product categories from star performers.
* **LLM API Integration:** Built a Python-to-Groq pipeline that extracts raw summary statistics and dynamically generates human-readable executive insights.
