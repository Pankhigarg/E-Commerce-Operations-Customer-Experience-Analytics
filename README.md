# E-Commerce-Supply-Chain-Performance-Analytics

## 📌 The Business Problem
Olist, a major Brazilian e-commerce platform, was experiencing fluctuating customer satisfaction scores. This project analyzes over 100,000 anonymized orders to identify the root causes of negative reviews, map the geographic distribution of revenue, and expose critical vulnerabilities in the logistics and delivery pipeline.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
* **Data Engineering & EDA:** Python (Pandas, NumPy), SQL Server
* **AI Integration:** Groq API (LLM for automated executive summaries)
* **Business Intelligence:** Power BI, DAX, Power Query

---

## ✨ Features

- Built an end-to-end analytics pipeline using Python, SQL Server, and Power BI.
- Engineered a star-schema data model for enterprise reporting.
- Developed 10 SQL business queries for operational analysis.
- Created interactive Power BI dashboards with DAX measures and synced slicers.
- Automated executive insight generation using Groq LLM integration.
- Identified critical business risks affecting customer satisfaction and retention.

## 🤖 AI-Generated Executive Summary

*These insights were automatically generated by passing Python EDA summary statistics to the Groq API.*

**1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY**  
- **Revenue & Order Volume**: Total revenue of BRL 15.4 M was generated from 96,470 delivered orders, yielding an average order value of roughly BRL 160.  
- **Delivery Performance**: The average delivery time is 12.1 days, with 6.8 % of orders arriving late. The median delivery time (10 days) is lower than the mean, indicating a right‑skewed distribution with a minority of very long‑haul deliveries.  
- **Customer Behavior**: 97 % of customers made only a single purchase, suggesting low repeat‑purchase rates.  
- **Seller Processing**: Median seller processing time is 1 day, indicating that most sellers prepare orders quickly.  
- **Top Revenue Segments**: Health & beauty, watches & gifts, and bed‑bath‑table categories drive the majority of sales.

---

**2. REVENUE INSIGHTS**  
- **Category Concentration**: The three top categories account for the bulk of revenue, implying that marketing spend and inventory focus should be aligned with these segments.  
- **Order Value Stability**: With an average order value of ~BRL 160, the platform is attracting mid‑tier shoppers; there is limited evidence of high‑ticket or bulk purchases.  
- **Revenue per Order**: The revenue per delivered order (BRL 15.4 M / 96,470) confirms that the platform is monetizing each transaction efficiently, but the high single‑purchase rate may cap long‑term revenue growth.

---

**3. CUSTOMER INSIGHTS**  
- **Low Repeat Purchase Rate**: 97 % single‑order customers indicate a high churn or low loyalty.  
- **Retention Opportunity**: The platform has a large base of one‑time buyers; targeted retention campaigns could unlock additional revenue.  
- **Customer Concentration**: No data on high‑spending customers is provided, but the single‑purchase dominance suggests that revenue is spread across many small buyers rather than a few big spenders.

---

**4. DELIVERY & OPERATIONS INSIGHTS**  
- **Late Deliveries**: 6.8 % of orders are late, which may erode trust and increase returns or cancellations.  
- **Delivery Time Distribution**: The mean (12.1 days) exceeds the median (10 days), indicating that a subset of orders experiences significant delays.  
- **Seller Processing Efficiency**: Median processing of 1 day is strong; however, the delivery lag suggests bottlenecks lie downstream (logistics, carrier, or fulfillment center).  
- **Operational Risk**: The combination of late deliveries and a high proportion of single‑order customers could signal a fragile supply chain that may not support scaling without addressing delivery reliability.

---

**5. CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE**  
- **Review Score**: An overall average review score of 4.16 (out of 5) is healthy but leaves room for improvement.  
- **Implication**: The score suggests customers are generally satisfied, yet the presence of late deliveries may be pulling the average down. Enhancing delivery reliability could lift the score further.

---

**6. KEY BUSINESS RISKS**  
1. **Delivery Reliability** – 6.8 % late deliveries risk customer dissatisfaction and churn.  
2. **Low Repeat Purchase Rate** – 97 % single‑order customers limit lifetime value and revenue growth.  
3. **Category Concentration** – Heavy reliance on three categories exposes the business to market shifts or supplier disruptions.  
4. **Operational Bottleneck** – The discrepancy between fast seller processing and slower overall delivery indicates potential logistic inefficiencies.

## 🧠 Key Technical Engineering Feats
* **Resolved DAX Circular Dependencies:** Pushed complex chronological sorting logic upstream to Power Query to ensure stable data modeling for operational delay buckets.
* **Granular Filtering Logic:** Utilized `AVERAGEX` and strict boolean filtering (`is_late = True`) to calculate true operational delay metrics without skewing baseline logistics data.
* **Strategic Quadrant Mapping:** Engineered customized scatter plots with hard-coded operational baselines (Revenue & Satisfaction) to instantly isolate high-risk product categories from star performers.
* **LLM API Integration:** Built a Python-to-Groq pipeline that extracts raw summary statistics and dynamically generates human-readable executive insights.
